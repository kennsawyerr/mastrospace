How Much Does Post-Construction Cleaning Cost in Port Harcourt?

Mastrospace · 6 min read

The honest answer is that it depends, and anyone who quotes a flat figure without seeing the building is guessing. Prices differ widely because no two finished buildings are in the same state. What we can do is explain what drives the cost, so you can judge any quote you receive.

What post-construction cleaning actually involves

This is not ordinary cleaning. After building or renovation, surfaces carry cement splashes, paint drips, adhesive, fine dust in every corner, stickers on glass and debris in places you would not expect. The work is usually done in stages:

  • Rough clean: removing debris, packaging and large waste while work is wrapping up.
  • Detailed clean: scraping, dusting and washing surfaces, windows, fittings, floors and cabinets.
  • Final clean: polishing and touch-ups so the space is ready to hand over or move into.

What decides the price

  • Size of the property: usually the starting point, often calculated by floor area.
  • Condition on arrival: heavy cement residue or paint overspray takes far longer than light dust.
  • Type of finishes: marble, tiles, glass, aluminium and polished wood each need different methods and care.
  • Number of floors and access: stairs, lifts and distance from the road affect time and logistics.
  • Exterior work: pressure washing the compound, walls, drive and balconies is typically separate.
  • Timeline: a tight deadline may need a larger crew at once.
  • Waste removal: hauling away rubble and bagged debris may or may not be included.

What a good quote should show

Ask for it in writing. It should state the areas covered, the number of cleaning stages, what equipment and materials are supplied, whether waste removal is included, how long the job will take, and what is excluded. If two quotes differ a lot, compare these details before you compare the totals.

Why the lowest quote can cost more

Cheap cleaning often skips the detailed stage, uses the wrong products on delicate surfaces, or leaves residue that scratches floors and fittings later. A repaint or a replaced window is far more expensive than doing the clean properly the first time.

How to keep costs under control

  • Ask your builder to remove bulk debris before cleaners arrive.
  • Book the cleaners before final painting and fittings are fully done, so the stages fit together.
  • Agree the scope in advance and do a walk-through before final payment.
